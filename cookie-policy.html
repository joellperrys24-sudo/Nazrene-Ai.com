Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Nazrene AI, London, United Kingdom

In short. This website sets no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no tracking cookies of any kind. There is nothing for you to opt into, which is why you will not see a cookie consent banner. The only cookies that may be present are strictly necessary security cookies set by our hosting provider.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website can store on your device. Cookies are used for many purposes — remembering that you are logged in, measuring how people use a site, or tracking you across other websites for advertising.

Related technologies include local storage and session storage, which work in a similar way but store data in your browser rather than as a file. This policy covers those too.

2. Cookies this website sets

We set none. Nazrene AI does not place cookies on your device, and does not use local storage or session storage for any purpose.

Specifically, this website does not use:

  • Analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site tracking technologies
  • Heat-mapping or session-recording tools
  • A/B testing or personalisation cookies

We do not build visitor profiles, and we do not follow you to other websites.

3. Cookies set by others

Even though we set no cookies ourselves, two categories of third party may set strictly necessary cookies as a function of serving and protecting this website.

Provider Purpose Category
Cloudflare
Website hosting and security		 Distinguishing legitimate visitors from automated traffic, bot mitigation and protecting the site against abuse. Cloudflare may set a security cookie such as __cf_bm for this purpose. Strictly necessary
Web3Forms
Enquiry form delivery		 Handling the submission of the enquiry form so your message reaches us. This service is only involved when you actively submit the form. Strictly necessary

Strictly necessary cookies are exempt from the consent requirement under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), because the website cannot operate securely without them. This is why no consent banner is shown.

4. Fonts and external resources

This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, your IP address is visible to Google as a normal part of serving the font. Google Fonts does not set cookies for this purpose.

If you would prefer no third-party requests at all, we can self-host the fonts. Contact us if this matters to your organisation.

5. Links to other websites

This website links to WhatsApp and LinkedIn. If you follow those links, you leave our site and the destination website's own cookie and privacy policies apply. We have no control over those, and we encourage you to read them.

6. How to control cookies

Because we set no cookies, there is nothing on this site to turn off. However, you can control cookies generally through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how this website functions — in particular, the security filtering that protects the enquiry form.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever introduce analytics, advertising or any other non-essential cookie, we will update this policy and add a consent mechanism before doing so. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
