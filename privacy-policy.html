Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Nazrene AI, London, United Kingdom

In short. This website collects personal information only when you choose to send us an enquiry. We use it to reply to you and nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not set cookies for marketing. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Nazrene AI ("we", "us", "our") is an AI automation, web security and privacy consultancy based in London, United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, we are the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Our contact details are listed in section 10 of this policy.

2. What information we collect

We collect personal information in two ways.

2.1 Information you give us

When you complete the enquiry form on this website, we ask for:

  • your full name
  • your company name
  • your business email address
  • your phone number
  • the size of your organisation
  • your company website address
  • the area of your business you would like to improve
  • the service you are interested in
  • an optional budget range
  • your preferred contact method
  • a description of your enquiry

The description field is free text. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in it — for example health information, financial account details, identity document numbers, or personal information about anyone other than yourself.

2.2 Information collected automatically

This website does not run analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioural tracking of any kind. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not track you across other websites.

Our hosting provider may record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request — in server logs for the purpose of operating and securing the service. This is a normal function of web hosting and is not used by us to identify individuals.

3. Why we use your information, and our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal information. We rely on the following:

PurposeLawful basis
Responding to your enquiry and discussing a potential engagement Consent (you tick the consent box before submitting), and legitimate interests in responding to a business enquiry addressed to us
Providing services you have engaged us for Performance of a contract
Keeping records of our communications and engagements Legitimate interests in running our business, and legal obligation where record-keeping is required by law
Protecting this website against abuse and spam Legitimate interests in maintaining a secure service

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of anything we did before you withdrew it.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Form submission processing. The enquiry form on this website is delivered by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form-handling service. Your submission passes through their systems so that it can be delivered to our inbox as an email.
  • Website hosting. This website is served by Cloudflare, which provides content delivery and security filtering.
  • Email delivery. Enquiries arrive in our email inbox, provided by Google.

We may also disclose personal information where we are legally required to do so — for example in response to a valid request from a regulator, court or law enforcement body.

Each of these providers processes data under its own terms and, where applicable, standard contractual clauses for international transfers. Some are based outside the United Kingdom, which means your information may be transferred internationally. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the safeguards those providers have in place.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: retained for up to 12 months so we can pick up a conversation if you come back to us, then deleted.
  • Enquiries that become client engagements: retained for the duration of the engagement and for up to six years afterwards, to meet accounting and legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Server logs held by our hosting provider: retained according to that provider's standard retention period.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how we use your information — which is what this policy does.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectify information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your information, where there is no overriding legal reason for us to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Portability — receive information you gave us in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is our basis for processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or alteration. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) to this website, access controls on our systems, and limiting the personal information we collect to what we actually need.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We describe our safeguards accurately rather than making absolute promises.

8. Cookies

This website does not set cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not use any third-party tracking scripts. Because of this, you will not see a cookie consent banner — there is nothing to consent to.

Our hosting and security provider may set strictly necessary technical cookies to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated traffic. These are essential to operating the service securely and do not track you across other websites.

Full detail is in our Cookie Policy.

9. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
