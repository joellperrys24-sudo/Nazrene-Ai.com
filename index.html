The commercial reality

Every missed enquiry is a missed opportunity.

Property businesses do not usually lose deals because of price. They lose them because the enquiry arrived at the wrong moment and nobody was there to answer it.

Calls go unanswered When the team is out on viewings, new enquiries ring out and move on to the next agency.
Enquiries arrive out of hours Evening and weekend interest is often the strongest, and it is exactly when nobody is watching the inbox.
Response times slip A reply that takes hours reads as disinterest, however good the property is.
Qualified leads go cold Interest fades quickly. A lead that was warm on Tuesday is often gone by Friday.
Qualification is manual Hours spent asking the same questions of everyone, including people who were never going to proceed.
Viewings are missed When nobody owns booking, the diary develops gaps that were entirely avoidable.
Admin repeatedly eats the day Copying details between systems, chasing confirmations, writing the same replies.
Follow-up is inconsistent Whether a lead gets chased depends on who happened to pick it up and how busy they were.
Sensitive data is handled loosely Applicant and landlord information travels through inboxes, spreadsheets and chat threads.
AI systems can be manipulated Public-facing assistants can be talked into ignoring their instructions and leaking what they should not.
Voice systems attract spam Automated callers burn through capacity meant for real applicants.
The website goes down Traffic spikes and malicious floods take your listings offline at the worst possible moment.
